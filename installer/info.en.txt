HaruMimi — before you install

• The developer collects no personal data. Your voice is processed only on this PC.
• Nothing is sent over the internet without your consent: online translation services and update checks are optional, and every download (speech models, offline translation model, updates) asks first and shows its size.
• Settings and downloaded models are stored in your user folder (%APPDATA%\RabbitHaru). Uninstalling can remove them too.
• The program is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning. This is expected for a small open-source project.

Source code and privacy notice: https://github.com/RabbitHaru/HaruMimi
